    How it works

    Three steps, no shipping

    Everything happens between students at the same university, so handovers take minutes, not days.

    1. 01

      Post it

      Snap a photo, add a price and condition, and tick "open to swaps" if you'd trade it.

    2. 02

      Connect

      Chat with verified students from your university right in CampusSwap — no phone numbers, no strangers.

    3. 03

      Meet on campus

      Pick a busy spot like the library entrance or student union, check the item, then pay or swap in person.

    Swap, don't spend

    Trade last semester's books for this semester's.

    Mark any listing as open to swaps and other students can offer an item instead of cash — or an item plus a little money to even it out.

    Offer an item instead of cash, or an item plus a little money.

    • Send a swap offer from any listing with the “Swap available” tag
    • Add cash on top when the items aren't worth the same
    • Both sides confirm the swap once you've met
