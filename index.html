How it works

Three steps, no shipping

Everything happens between students at the same university, so handovers take minutes, not days.

  1. 01

    Post it

    Snap a photo, add a price and condition, and tick "open to swaps" if you'd trade it.

  2. 02

    Connect

    Chat with verified students from your university right in CampusSwap — no phone numbers, no strangers.

  3. 03

    Meet on campus

    Pick a busy spot like the library entrance or student union, check the item, then pay or swap in person.
